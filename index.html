Eko Geko

A card-based game promoting sustainability, teamwork and innovation.

About the Game

Eko Geko was created by ECIU student ambassadors to support sustainability learning through play, teamwork and innovation.

Educational Purpose

The game encourages players to discuss challenges, collaborate, exchange ideas and explore sustainable solutions.
